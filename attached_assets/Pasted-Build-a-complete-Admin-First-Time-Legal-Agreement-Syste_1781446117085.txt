Build a complete Admin First-Time Legal Agreement System for my multi-store platform.

OBJECTIVE

When an Admin logs in for the very first time, they must complete and accept 4 legal pages before accessing any dashboard, store, products, loyalty card, Virtual Try-On, rewards, or other platform features.

The 4 required pages are:

1. Terms & Conditions
2. Privacy Policy
3. Refund Policy
4. Disclaimer

Admin must accept all 4 pages before account setup is completed.

--------------------------------------------------

FIRST LOGIN FLOW

When a newly created Admin logs in for the first time:

• Automatically redirect to Legal Agreement Setup.
• Lock all dashboard pages until setup is completed.
• Prevent bypassing through URL manipulation.
• Show progress bar:

Step 1 of 4 → Terms & Conditions
Step 2 of 4 → Privacy Policy
Step 3 of 4 → Refund Policy
Step 4 of 4 → Disclaimer

Admin cannot continue until the checkbox on the current page is selected.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 1 – TERMS & CONDITIONS

Title:
Terms & Conditions

Show professional legal content.

Content must clearly state:

• The platform only provides store management technology.
• The platform is not a seller of products.
• The store owner/admin is fully responsible for all listed products.
• The store owner/admin is responsible for product quality, pricing, descriptions, images, and customer communication.
• Illegal, counterfeit, copyrighted, misleading, or prohibited products are not allowed.
• The platform is not responsible for customer complaints or disputes.
• The platform is not responsible for business losses.
• The platform may suspend or terminate accounts violating platform policies.
• The admin is responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations.

Required Checkbox:

☐ I have read and agree to the Terms & Conditions.

Next button remains disabled until checked.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 2 – PRIVACY POLICY

Title:
Privacy Policy

Content must state:

• The platform stores account information.
• The platform stores product information uploaded by admins.
• The platform stores loyalty card data.
• The platform stores Virtual Try-On related information when required.
• Analytics may be used to improve platform performance.
• Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information.
• Admin data will not be sold to third parties.
• Data may be used for platform functionality and support purposes.

Required Checkbox:

☐ I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy.

Next button remains disabled until checked.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 3 – REFUND POLICY

Title:
Refund Policy

Content must state:

• Subscription or platform fees are charged for software access.
• Fees are generally non-refundable.
• Refund requests may be reviewed individually.
• Violation of platform policies may result in account suspension or termination.
• Platform owner reserves the right to approve or reject refund requests.

Required Checkbox:

☐ I have read and agree to the Refund Policy.

Next button remains disabled until checked.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 4 – DISCLAIMER

Title:
Disclaimer

Content must state:

• The platform only provides technology services.
• The platform does not sell products directly.
• The platform does not guarantee sales, customers, revenue, or profits.
• Virtual Try-On results are AI-generated previews.
• Actual product appearance may differ from AI previews.
• The platform is not liable for customer claims against store owners.
• The platform is not responsible for product accuracy or product performance.
• The platform is not responsible for third-party actions.

Required Checkbox:

☐ I have read and agree to the Disclaimer.

Next button remains disabled until checked.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL CONFIRMATION PAGE

After all 4 pages are accepted:

Display confirmation message:

"I confirm that I have read, understood, and accepted all platform policies."

Required Checkbox:

☐ I Agree To All Policies

Button:

[ Complete Setup ]

Then:

• Never show these legal pages again.
• Open dashboard directly.

IF final_acceptance = FALSE

Then:

• Force admin to complete legal agreement setup before accessing the platform.
